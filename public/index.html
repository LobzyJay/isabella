LAGOS · ANNO DOMINI MMXXVI
To the First Fifty

Isabella de’ Medici was a Florentine noblewoman of the Renaissance, known for her wit, her music and her patronage of the arts. History came close to forgetting her.

We gave this tower her name so that Lagos remembers her every day. Fifty-five storeys over Victoria Island, run as a hotel, and owned first by fifty people who chose to be early.

I am obsessed with proving that Africans can build the best luxury in the world. Isabella is not my next project. It is my masterpiece.

Dr. Sijibomi Ogundele
Group Managing Director, Sujimoto Group
